GAME: Crazy Eights (also known as Switch, Swedish Rummy, Last One, Mau-Mau)
SOURCE: https://www.pagat.com/eights/crazy8s.html

=== PLAYERS & DECK ===
- 2 or more players
- Standard 52-card deck (or two decks shuffled for many players)
- Card values for scoring: 8 = 50 pts; J/Q/K = 10 pts; Ace = 1 pt; others = face value

=== DEAL ===
- 2 players: 7 cards each
- 3+ players: 5 cards each
- Remaining cards = Stock (face down)
- Top card of stock turned face up to start the Discard Pile

=== OBJECTIVE ===
Be the first to get rid of all cards in your hand. Others score penalty points for remaining cards.

=== PLAY (Each Turn) ===
Starting with player to dealer's left (clockwise), each player must either:
(A) Play a legal card face up on the Discard Pile, OR
(B) Draw a card from the Stock

=== LEGAL PLAYS ===
1. If top card is NOT an Eight: play any card matching the RANK or SUIT of the top discard
   (e.g., top card = K♥ → can play any King or any Heart)
2. An Eight can be played on ANY card regardless of suit or rank
   - Player of the Eight MUST nominate a suit
3. If top card IS an Eight: must play another Eight OR any card of the NOMINATED suit

=== SPECIAL CARDS (common house rules) ===
- Two (2): Next player must draw 2 cards (or play another Two to pass it on)
- Queen (or Jack in some variants): Next player is skipped (misses a turn)
- Ace: Reverses direction of play (play becomes anti-clockwise)
- Eight: Wild card — play on anything, nominate a new suit

=== FIRST CARD RULE ===
- If an Eight is turned up as the starting card, dealer nominates a suit
- If a special card is turned up, its effect is applied immediately

=== SAYING "LAST CARD" ===
- Player with one card remaining MUST say "last card" before the next player takes a turn
- Failure to do so = draw 2 cards as penalty

=== STOCK EXHAUSTED ===
- Shuffle all played cards EXCEPT the top discard card to form a new stock

=== WINNING ===
- First player to get rid of all cards wins
- Other players score penalty points for remaining cards:
  - Eight: 50 pts; Face cards (J/Q/K): 10 pts; Ace: 1 pt; 2-10: face value

=== VARIATIONS ===
- Drawing: Some rules require you to KEEP drawing until you can play (vs. draw once)
- Mandatory play: Some rules say you MUST play if you can (no voluntary drawing)
- Multiple equal cards: Some allow playing all cards of the same rank at once
- Crazy Eights Countdown variant: Players start with score of 8 (8 cards); each time you go out reduce score by 1 and get new cards; first to reach 0 wins

=== KEY CONSTRAINTS (PARTIAL OBSERVABILITY) ===
- All player hands are hidden; only current Discard Pile top card and draw count are observable
- When special cards are played, effects are immediately visible
- Players can count cards drawn from stock by others to estimate hand sizes
- Nominated suit (after Eight is played) is public information
- "Last card" declarations are public signals
