GAME: Gin Rummy
SOURCE: https://www.pagat.com/rummy/ginrummy.html

=== PLAYERS & DECK ===
- Primarily 2 players (3-4 player variants exist)
- One standard 52-card deck
- Card values: A=1, 2-10=face value, J/Q/K=10

=== DEAL ===
- Each player receives 10 cards
- 21st card turned face up to start the Discard Pile
- Remaining deck is the Stock (face down)
- First dealer: player who draws lower card; thereafter winner of previous hand deals

=== OBJECTIVE ===
Form as many cards as possible into valid combinations (sets/runs) to minimise "deadwood" (unmatched cards).
- Run/Sequence: 3+ consecutive cards of the same suit (e.g., 4♦-5♦-6♦)
- Set/Group: 3-4 cards of the same rank (e.g., 7♣-7♦-7♠)
- Ace is always LOW: A-2-3 is valid; A-K-Q is NOT valid
- A card can only belong to ONE combination at a time

=== PLAY (Each Turn) ===
1. DRAW: Take the top card from Stock OR the top card from Discard Pile
   - Cannot take the top discard and return the same card in the same turn
2. DISCARD: Place one card face up on top of the Discard Pile

=== FIRST TURN SPECIAL RULE ===
- Non-dealer first has the option to take the turned-up card
- If non-dealer declines, dealer may take the card
- If both decline, non-dealer draws from stock and play begins normally

=== KNOCKING ===
- After drawing, a player may "knock" by:
  1. Discarding one card face down
  2. Exposing the entire hand arranged into sets/runs
  3. Total deadwood value must be 10 points or less to knock
- Opponent then exposes their hand and (if knocker didn't go GIN) may lay off unmatched cards onto knocker's melds
- Cards cannot be laid off on the knocker's deadwood

=== GOING GIN ===
- Knocking with ZERO deadwood = "Going Gin"
- Bonus 20 points awarded; opponent cannot lay off any cards
- Player going Gin can NEVER be undercut

=== SCORING ===
- Knocker's deadwood < Opponent's deadwood: Knocker scores the difference
- Knocker's deadwood >= Opponent's deadwood (undercut): Opponent scores the difference + 10-point bonus
- Going Gin: 20-point bonus + opponent's deadwood score
- Game ends when one player reaches 100 cumulative points; that player gets +100 bonus
- Each player also gets +20 points ("box bonus") per hand won
- Loser scores 0 all game → winner's bonus doubles to 200

=== PLAY END CONDITIONS ===
- Stock reduced to 2 cards and player who took 3rd-last card discards without knocking → hand cancelled, no score

=== KEY CONSTRAINTS (PARTIAL OBSERVABILITY) ===
- Only the top card of the Discard Pile is visible; Stock is hidden
- Opponent's entire hand is unknown until knocking occurs
- Players can track discards to infer opponent strategy
- Deadwood count of opponent is unknown until showdown
