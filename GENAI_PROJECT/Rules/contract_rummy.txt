GAME: Contract Rummy (also known as Liverpool Rummy / May I? / Shanghai Rummy / Progressive Rummy)
SOURCE: https://www.pagat.com/rummy/ctrummy.html

=== PLAYERS & DECK ===
- 3 to 5 players (optimum: 4)
- Two standard 52-card decks + jokers
- Number of jokers = (number of players - 1)
  → 3 players: 1 joker (106 cards total); 4 players: 2 jokers (107 total); 5 players: 3 jokers (108 total)

=== DEAL ===
- 7 rounds total
- Rounds 1-3: Each player receives 10 cards
- Rounds 4-7: Each player receives 12 cards
- Remaining cards form the Stock; top card turned face up starts the Discard Pile

=== OBJECTIVE ===
Lowest cumulative score at the end of 7 rounds wins.

=== MELD TYPES ===
- Sequence/Run: 4+ consecutive cards of the same suit (e.g., 4♥-5♥-6♥-7♥)
  - Ace can be high or low (A-2-3-4 and J-Q-K-A valid; Q-K-A-2 NOT valid)
- Group/Set/Book: 3+ cards of the same rank (any suit, duplicates allowed)
- Once melded, sequences can be extended at either end; groups can be extended with same-rank cards

=== CONTRACTS (Per Round) ===
Round 1: 2 Groups of 3 (6 cards)
Round 2: 1 Group of 3 + 1 Sequence of 4 (7 cards)
Round 3: 2 Sequences of 4 (8 cards)
Round 4: 3 Groups of 3 (9 cards)
Round 5: 2 Groups of 3 + 1 Sequence of 4 (10 cards)
Round 6: 1 Group of 3 + 2 Sequences of 4 (11 cards)
Round 7: 3 Sequences of 4 — NO discard allowed (player must meld ALL remaining cards)

Must fulfill contract BEFORE any melding or laying off is allowed.

=== PLAY (Each Turn) ===
1. DRAW: Take top card from Stock OR top card from Discard Pile
2. MELD (optional): Lay down contract + additional cards if desired
3. LAY OFF (optional, only after contract laid down): Add cards to existing melds
4. DISCARD: One card face up (except in Round 7's final meld)

=== "MAY I?" — TAKING DISCARD OUT OF TURN ===
- If the current player draws from stock, other players may request the top discard ("May I?")
- Priority: player whose turn is soonest after the drawing player gets priority
- Penalty: calling player takes the discard AND draws 1 extra card from stock
- After one "May I?" is resolved, the next card on top can be "May I?"-ed by a different player
- The same player cannot take two consecutive out-of-turn discards

=== JOKERS ===
- Can substitute for any missing card in sequences or groups
- Joker in a SEQUENCE: any player who has melded their contract AND holds the real card may swap it for the joker (must lay off joker immediately — cannot save for later)
- Joker in a GROUP: considered "dead" — cannot be reclaimed

=== SCORING (Penalty Points for cards remaining in hand) ===
- Joker: 15 pts (25 pts in May I? variant)
- Ace: 11 pts
- Face cards (K, Q, J): 10 pts each
- Number cards: face value
Lowest total score at end of 7 rounds wins.

=== KEY CONSTRAINTS (PARTIAL OBSERVABILITY) ===
- Opponent hands are hidden; only discards are public
- "May I?" requests reveal that a player wants a specific card type
- Number of jokers available is public knowledge
- Melds on the table are visible to all players
- Stock size can be estimated but not exactly known
