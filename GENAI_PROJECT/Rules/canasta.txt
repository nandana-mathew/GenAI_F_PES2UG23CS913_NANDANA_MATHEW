GAME: Canasta (Classic Canasta)
SOURCE: https://www.pagat.com/rummy/canasta.html

=== PLAYERS & DECK ===
- Best for 4 players in fixed partnerships (2 vs 2)
- Two standard 52-card decks + 4 jokers = 108 cards
- Natural cards: A, K, Q, J, 10, 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4
- Wild cards: ALL twos (deuces) and ALL jokers
- Threes: special functions (not melded normally)

=== CARD VALUES (for scoring) ===
- Joker: 50 pts; Ace/Two: 20 pts; K/Q/J/10/9/8: 10 pts; 7/6/5/4/Black3: 5 pts; Red 3: bonus

=== DEAL ===
- 11 cards dealt to each player
- Top card of remaining stock turned face up to start Discard Pile
- If first face-up card is wild or red three, another card is turned until a normal card appears (pile is "frozen")
- Red threes dealt to players must be placed face up immediately; replacement cards drawn

=== OBJECTIVE ===
Score points by melding cards. Complete 7-card melds ("Canastas") for bonuses.
A valid meld: 3+ cards of the same natural rank (4 through Ace); wild cards may substitute with restrictions.

=== MELD RULES ===
- Every meld must contain at least 2 natural cards
- Maximum 3 wild cards in any single meld
- No meld can contain more than 3 wild cards
- Partnership cannot have two separate melds of the same rank (auto-merged)
- Cannot add wild cards to a pure canasta to convert it (no — wild cards CAN be added to "dirty" canastas up to limit)

=== CANASTAS ===
- Canasta = any meld of exactly 7+ cards
- Natural/Pure/Red Canasta: all 7 cards are natural (no wild cards) → topped with a RED card; bonus = 500 pts
- Mixed/Dirty/Black Canasta: 1+ wild cards included → topped with a black card; bonus = 300 pts

=== PLAY (Each Turn) ===
1. DRAW: Take top card from Stock OR take the ENTIRE Discard Pile (if conditions met)
2. MELD (optional, but MANDATORY if taking discard pile)
3. DISCARD: One card face up on Discard Pile

=== TAKING THE DISCARD PILE ===
If pile is NOT frozen: can take it if you can meld its top card using:
   (a) Two natural cards of same rank as top discard, OR
   (b) Top discard matches rank of an existing meld (add it to that meld)
If pile IS frozen: must hold two natural cards of same rank as top discard to take it

=== FROZEN DISCARD PILE ===
Pile becomes frozen in three cases:
1. Wild card in the pile (placed at right-angle)
2. Red three turned up as first discard
3. Your partnership has not yet melded (frozen for you only)

=== INITIAL MELD REQUIREMENT ===
- First meld by a partnership must meet a minimum card value count
- Requirement depends on cumulative score: 
  Below 0: 15 pts; 0-1499: 50 pts; 1500-2999: 90 pts; 3000+: 120 pts
- Can use top of discard pile + hand cards to meet count
- Bonuses (canastas, red threes) do NOT count toward minimum

=== GOING OUT ===
- Can only go out if partnership has at least ONE completed canasta
- Go out by melding all cards (with or without a final discard)
- Optional: ask partner "may I go out?" before going out; partner must answer yes/no and both are bound

=== SCORING ===
Hand score = (Bonus points) + (Value of melded cards) - (Value of cards in hand)
Bonuses:
- Natural canasta: 500 pts each
- Mixed canasta: 300 pts each
- Going out: 100 pts (200 if concealed)
- Going out concealed: additional 100 pts
- Red threes: 100 pts each (400 pts if all 4; 800 if team has no canasta, these become penalties)
Game ends when one partnership reaches 5000 cumulative points.

=== KEY CONSTRAINTS (PARTIAL OBSERVABILITY) ===
- Partner's hand is private (cannot show or discuss cards)
- Opponent hands are completely hidden
- Discard pile is visible (face up); Stock cards are hidden
- Once pile is taken, opponent can count approximately how many cards were in it
- Wild cards are visible in the pile (frozen indication)
